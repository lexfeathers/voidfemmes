My latest song _Faltering Stair_ has just released on [Bandcamp](https://voidfemmes.bandcamp.com/track/faltering-stair) and [Ampwall.](https://ampwall.com/a/voidfemmes/album/faltering-stair) This is the first "pop" song I've released under the Void Femmes name, I think, and I'm excited to get it into your hands! I had a lot of fun rethinking my approach to vocals in this one, and the guitar parts were a blast to record.

I hope you like it!
