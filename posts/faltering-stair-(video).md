This is a bit of a different speed, but I made a music video for once! It's for my new song [Faltering Stair](../faltering-stair-(single)), which also released today on Bandcamp and Ampwall.

I treated this as a challenge to make something as well as I could, and in as short a time as possible. I spent all of Saturday planning, filming and editing, and wrapped up the final edit by Sunday evening. This only worked because the idea is dead simple, and I had a lot of fun doing it!
